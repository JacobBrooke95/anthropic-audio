Today Anthropic, PBC announced the closing of its initial public offering of an aggregate of 1,127,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock at a public offering price of $83.00 per share, including the full exercise by the underwriters of their option to purchase an additional 147,000,000 shares of Class A common stock from Anthropic at the initial public offering price. The offering consisted of 1,103,351,354 shares sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 shares sold by certain selling stockholders. The aggregate gross proceeds from the offering, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses, were approximately $93.5 billion, of which approximately $91.6 billion was received by Anthropic. Anthropic did not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders.


Anthropic’s Class A common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol “ANT.”


In addition to the shares of Class A common stock sold in the initial public offering, Anthropic closed the concurrent sale of an aggregate of 313,253,007 shares of Class N common stock to Advanced Micro Devices, Inc., Broadcom Inc., Google LLC, Micron Technology, Inc., Microsoft Corporation, NVIDIA Corporation, and TSMC Partners, Ltd. at the public offering price of $83.00 per share, in a private placement for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $26.0 billion. The sale of these shares of Class N common stock was not registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”). Aggregate gross proceeds from the initial public offering, including the shares sold by the selling stockholders, and the concurrent private placement were approximately $119.5 billion, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses.


Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC and Morgan Stanley acted as lead book-running managers for the offering. J.P. Morgan, Citigroup, Barclays, Wells Fargo Securities, BofA Securities, Deutsche Bank Securities, RBC Capital Markets, and UBS Investment Bank acted as book-running managers for the offering. BMO Capital Markets, BNP Paribas, Credit Agricole CIB, Jefferies, Mizuho, MUFG, and TD Securities acted as additional book-running managers for the offering. BTIG, Cantor, Capital One Securities, Macquarie Capital, and Societe Generale acted as co-managers for the offering.


A registration statement relating to these securities was declared effective by the Securities and Exchange Commission on November 18, 2026, and a related registration statement filed pursuant to Rule 462(b) under the Securities Act became effective upon filing on November 18, 2026. The offering was made only by means of a prospectus. Copies of the final prospectus may be obtained from: Goldman Sachs & Co. LLC, Attention: Prospectus Department, 200 West Street, New York, NY 10282, by telephone at (866) 471-2526, or by email at prospectus-ny@ny.email.gs.com; or Morgan Stanley & Co. LLC, Attention: Prospectus Department, 180 Varick Street, 2nd Floor, New York, NY 10014, or by email at prospectus@morganstanley.com.


Today’s announcement does not constitute an offer to sell or the solicitation of an offer to buy these securities, nor shall there be any sale of these securities in any state or jurisdiction in which such offer, solicitation, or sale would be unlawful prior to registration or qualification under the securities laws of any such state or jurisdiction.
