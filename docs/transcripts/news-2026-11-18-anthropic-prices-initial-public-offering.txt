Anthropic prices initial public offering.

From Anthropic Newsroom, published November 18, 2026.

Today Anthropic, P B C priced its initial public offering of its Class A common stock at 83.00 dollarsper share. The offering consists of 980000000 shares of Class A common stock, 956351354 of which are being sold by Anthropic and 23648646 of which are being sold by certain selling stockholders. Anthropic will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders.

The shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol “ANT.” The offering is expected to close on November 20, 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. In addition, Anthropic has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 147000000 shares of its Class A common stock.
